What is Sports Recovery Massage?

Sports recovery massage is a specialized therapeutic technique that focuses on reducing muscle soreness, improving circulation, and accelerating the body's natural healing processes after physical activity. Unlike general relaxation massage, sports recovery massage is targeted and outcome-oriented—it's designed to help you get back to training faster.

Benefits of Sports Recovery Massage

  • Reduces Muscle Soreness: Alleviates DOMS (Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness) after intense workouts
  • Speeds Recovery: Improves blood flow and removes metabolic waste from muscles
  • Prevents Injury: Identifies and treats tight muscles before they become problems
  • Improves Sleep: Promotes deep, restorative sleep essential for athletic recovery
  • Mental Reset: Reduces stress and improves focus for competition
  • Enhances Performance: Prepares muscles for optimal function
  • Reduces Inflammation: Helps manage post-workout inflammation
  • Breaks Down Adhesions: Releases scar tissue and fascial restrictions

Techniques We Use for Sports Recovery

  • Deep Tissue Massage: Releases chronic tension in overworked muscles
  • Myofascial Release: Stretches tight fascia and improves mobility
  • Trigger Point Therapy: Relieves specific pain points and referred pain
  • Active and Passive Stretching: Improves flexibility and range of motion
  • Cross-Fiber Friction: Breaks down adhesions and scar tissue
  • Lymphatic Drainage: Reduces swelling and inflammation post-activity
  • Cupping Therapy: Optional add-on for deeper tissue work

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sports recovery massage?

Sports recovery massage focuses on reducing muscle soreness, improving circulation, and accelerating the body's natural healing processes after physical activity.

How does sports recovery massage help with DOMS?

It alleviates Delayed Onset Muscle Soreness by improving blood flow, removing metabolic waste, and promoting relaxation of tight muscles.

Do you offer mobile sports recovery massage in Bellevue?

Yes! We bring the table to your home or office in Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, and the Eastside. Perfect for post-workout recovery.

How often should I get sports recovery massage?

For active athletes, 1-2 times per week is recommended. For recreational athletes, weekly or bi-weekly sessions can significantly improve recovery.

When is the best time to get a sports recovery massage?

Within 24-48 hours after intense exercise is ideal. Regular maintenance sessions are also beneficial for preventing injuries.

What's the difference between sports recovery massage and regular massage?

Sports recovery massage specifically targets muscle groups used in your sport, focuses on reducing DOMS, and includes techniques that speed athletic recovery.

Can sports recovery massage help prevent injuries?

Yes! By identifying and treating tight muscles, imbalances, and restrictions, sports recovery massage helps prevent common athletic injuries.
