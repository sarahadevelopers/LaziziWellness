Benefits of Sports Massage

  • Faster Recovery: Reduces muscle soreness and fatigue after intense training
  • Injury Prevention: Identifies and addresses muscular imbalances before they become problems
  • Improved Performance: Increases flexibility, range of motion, and muscle efficiency
  • Pain Relief: Relieves chronic muscle tension and trigger points
  • Mental Focus: Reduces stress and improves concentration for competition
  • Enhanced Circulation: Improves blood flow to muscles, delivering oxygen and nutrients
  • Reduced DOMS: Minimizes delayed onset muscle soreness post-workout

Sports Massage Techniques We Use

  • Deep Tissue Massage: Targets deep muscle layers for chronic tension and adhesions
  • Myofascial Release: Releases tight fascia and improves mobility
  • Trigger Point Therapy: Relieves specific pain points and referred pain
  • Active and Passive Stretching: Improves flexibility and range of motion
  • Cross-Fiber Friction: Breaks down scar tissue and adhesions
  • Cupping Therapy: Optional add-on for deeper tissue work and increased circulation
  • Lymphatic Drainage: Reduces swelling and inflammation post-activity

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I get a sports massage?

Pre-event (1-2 days before), post-event (1-2 days after), or regularly for maintenance. We can create a schedule tailored to your training plan.

Is sports massage painful?

It can be moderately intense but shouldn't be unbearable. We adjust pressure to your comfort level and communicate throughout.

Do you offer mobile sports massage in Seattle?

Yes! We bring the table to your home, office, or even the field. Perfect for athletes who want to avoid travel after training.

Can sports massage help with muscle strains?

Yes. It promotes healing by increasing blood flow, reducing inflammation, and breaking down scar tissue.

How often should I get sports massage?

For competitive athletes, weekly sessions are common. For recreational athletes, bi-weekly or monthly sessions may be sufficient.

What's the difference between sports massage and deep tissue massage?

Sports massage is specifically tailored to athletic activities and performance goals. It includes stretching and movement techniques, while deep tissue focuses more on chronic tension release.

Do you offer sports massage for specific sports?

Yes! We specialize in massage for runners, cyclists, hikers, pickleball players, skiers, swimmers, weightlifters, and more. We tailor each session to your specific sport.
