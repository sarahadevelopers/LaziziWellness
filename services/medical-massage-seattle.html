What is Medical Massage?

Medical massage is prescribed massage therapy that targets specific medical conditions. It's often part of a comprehensive treatment plan that may include physical therapy, chiropractic care, or surgery recovery. Each session is documented, with measurable outcomes tracked over time to ensure progress.

Key Differences: Medical vs. Relaxation Massage

  • Outcome-Focused: Medical massage has specific treatment goals like pain reduction or improved mobility
  • Clinical Documentation: Sessions are documented and progress is tracked
  • Prescribed Care: Often recommended by doctors, physical therapists, or chiropractors
  • HSA/FSA Eligible: Medical massage qualifies for health savings accounts with a referral
  • Insurance Reimbursement: We provide superbills for insurance claims
  • Targeted Techniques: Specific techniques for specific conditions

What's the difference between medical and relaxation massage?

Medical massage targets specific conditions and is outcome-focused with clinical documentation. Relaxation massage is for stress relief and general well-being.

Can I get medical massage after surgery?

Yes, with your surgeon's approval. We specialize in post-surgery rehabilitation massage to reduce scar tissue and restore range of motion.

What conditions can medical massage help with?

Do I need a doctor's referral for medical massage?

Not necessarily. You can book medical massage without a referral. However, a referral is required for HSA/FSA and insurance reimbursement.
