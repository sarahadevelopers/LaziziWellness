What is a Couples Massage?

A couples massage involves two therapists working on both partners simultaneously in the same room. You can select different styles—Swedish for relaxation, deep tissue for tension relief, or hot stone for deep warmth. Our therapists coordinate their techniques to create a harmonious experience for both of you.

Couples Massage Options
