What is Swedish Massage?

Swedish massage is the most well-known and widely practiced massage style worldwide. It uses long, flowing strokes, kneading, and circular movements to relax muscles, improve circulation, and reduce stress. The gentle, rhythmic pressure creates a deeply calming experience that soothes both body and mind.

What are the benefits of Swedish massage?

Swedish massage reduces stress, improves circulation, relieves muscle tension, improves sleep quality, boosts mood, and supports immune function.

What's the difference between Swedish and deep tissue massage?

Swedish massage uses gentle, flowing strokes for relaxation and stress relief. Deep tissue massage uses firmer pressure to target chronic tension and muscle knots.

How often should I get Swedish massage?

For stress relief and general wellness, once a month is common. For chronic tension, weekly or bi-weekly sessions can be beneficial.

What should I wear for a Swedish massage?

You'll undress to your comfort level. Professional draping ensures your privacy at all times. Most clients choose to undress completely or wear underwear.
