What is Lymphatic Drainage Massage?

Manual lymphatic drainage (MLD) is a specialized massage technique that stimulates the lymphatic system to remove metabolic waste, reduce inflammation, and promote healing. Unlike deep tissue massage, MLD uses light, rhythmic strokes that follow the natural flow of lymph fluid. The lymphatic system is responsible for removing toxins, waste products, and excess fluid from your tissues—when it becomes sluggish, you may experience swelling, fatigue, and a weakened immune system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get lymphatic drainage massage?

For general wellness, once a month. For post-surgery recovery, 2-3 times weekly for 2-4 weeks. For chronic conditions, weekly sessions may be recommended.

Does lymphatic drainage hurt?

No. It's a very gentle, soothing massage. You may feel slight pressure but no pain. It's completely different from deep tissue massage.

Can I get lymphatic drainage after surgery?

Yes, but wait 1-2 weeks post-surgery and get your surgeon's approval first. We'll work with your healthcare provider for the safest experience.

Do you offer mobile lymphatic drainage in Bellevue?

Yes! We bring the table to your home in Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Seattle, or anywhere in King County. Perfect for post-surgery patients who can't travel.

What should I do after lymphatic drainage massage?

Drink plenty of water, rest, and avoid strenuous activity. Some clients experience increased urination as the body releases excess fluid.

Is lymphatic drainage safe during pregnancy?

Yes, with your doctor's approval. Our therapists are trained in prenatal lymphatic drainage techniques that are safe for expecting mothers.
