Pregnancy is a beautiful journey, but it often comes with aches, pains, and stress. Prenatal massage in Bellevue, Seattle, and the Eastside from Lazizi® Wellness provides safe, nurturing care for expecting mothers. Our WA licensed therapists are specially trained in pregnancy massage techniques.

From lower back pain to sciatica, leg swelling to stress, our therapists understand the unique needs of pregnant bodies. We use specialized positioning with pregnancy pillows and gentle techniques to ensure your comfort and safety throughout every stage of your pregnancy.

Important: Always consult your doctor before receiving prenatal massage. We work with you and your healthcare provider for the safest experience.

Benefits of Prenatal Massage

  • Reduces Back & Hip Pain: Relieves pressure from your growing baby and changing posture
  • Improves Sleep: Promotes relaxation and better rest quality
  • Reduces Swelling: Helps manage edema in ankles, feet, and hands
  • Reduces Stress & Anxiety: Calms the nervous system and lowers cortisol
  • Prepares for Birth: Supports physical and emotional readiness for labor
  • Improves Circulation: Supports healthy blood flow for you and baby
  • Relieves Sciatica: Gentle techniques to ease nerve pain
  • Reduces Headaches: Tension relief for pregnancy-related headaches

When to Start Prenatal Massage

  • First Trimester (1-12 weeks): Light massage with doctor's approval
  • Second Trimester (13-27 weeks): Most therapists recommend starting now—this is the sweet spot
  • Third Trimester (28-40 weeks): Many women find weekly sessions very helpful for pain and stress relief
  • Postpartum: Gentle massage can aid recovery after birth

What to Expect During Your Prenatal Massage

  • Safe Positioning: We use specialized pregnancy pillows to keep you comfortable in a side-lying position
  • Gentle Pressure: We avoid deep pressure on the lower back, abdomen, and certain acupressure points
  • Soothing Oils: Safe, natural, unscented or lightly scented oils for pregnancy
  • Flexible Options: Side-lying, semi-reclining, or seated—whatever is most comfortable for you
  • Professional Draping: Your privacy is always respected

Frequently Asked Questions

Is prenatal massage safe during pregnancy?

Yes, with your doctor's approval and a trained therapist. We follow strict prenatal massage protocols and use safe positioning.

When should I start prenatal massage?

Most therapists recommend starting in the second trimester (around 13-14 weeks). Always check with your doctor first.

Do you offer mobile prenatal massage in Bellevue?

Yes! We bring the table and pregnancy pillows to your Bellevue or Seattle home. Perfect for expectant mothers who want to avoid travel.

What areas do you avoid during prenatal massage?

We avoid deep pressure on the lower back, abdomen, and certain acupressure points. We always use professional draping and positioning.

Can prenatal massage help with sciatica during pregnancy?

Yes! Our therapists are trained to safely relieve sciatic pain during pregnancy using gentle techniques that avoid pressure on the nerve.

Is it safe to have a massage in the third trimester?

Yes, with your doctor's approval. Many women find weekly sessions in the third trimester very helpful for pain and stress relief.

How often should I get prenatal massage?

Many women benefit from weekly sessions, especially in the third trimester. Some find bi-weekly sufficient. We'll help you find the right frequency for your needs.
