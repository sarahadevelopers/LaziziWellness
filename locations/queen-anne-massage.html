What a Mobile Massage Appointment Looks Like

1

We bring everything

Professional massage table, fresh linens, oils, music and sanitizer. You don't need to provide anything.

2

You provide a clear space

A roughly 10×10 foot area — living room, bedroom or home office works well. We'll confirm details before arrival.

3

Parking & building access

Let us know about parking or entry — a driveway, visitor spot or loading zone on the hill is ideal. For apartments and offices, share any gate, elevator or concierge instructions.

4

Aftercare & home guidance

We'll share simple stretches or self-care suggestions, then pack everything up. You rest — we clean up.
