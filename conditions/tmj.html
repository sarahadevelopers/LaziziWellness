What is TMJ Disorder?

TMJ disorder affects the joint that connects your jaw to your skull. When this joint becomes inflamed or the surrounding muscles become overly tight, you may experience:

  • Jaw pain or stiffness
  • Clicking, popping, or grinding sounds when opening your mouth
  • Headaches, especially in the temples
  • Ear pain or ringing (tinnitus)
  • Facial muscle fatigue
  • Neck and shoulder tension
  • Difficulty chewing or biting

How Massage Therapy Can Help with TMJ

Massage therapy may help support recovery from TMJ by:

  • Releasing tight jaw muscles: The masseter, temporalis, and pterygoid muscles often become hypertonic from stress or clenching.
  • Reducing trigger points: We apply sustained pressure to release painful knots that refer pain to the head and neck.
  • Improving circulation: Increased blood flow reduces inflammation and promotes tissue healing.
  • Calming the nervous system: Stress reduction helps break the cycle of clenching and grinding.

Our TMJ Treatment Approach

Our licensed therapists use a combination of techniques tailored to your specific needs:

  • Intraoral massage: Gentle work inside the mouth to release the pterygoid and masseter muscles
  • Extraoral massage: Massage of the jaw, temples, and neck
  • Myofascial release: Stretching the fascia surrounding the jaw and neck
  • Trigger point therapy: Targeting specific pain points
  • Postural correction: Addressing forward head posture that often contributes to TMJ

Who Can Benefit?

  • People with diagnosed TMJ disorder
  • Those who grind or clench their teeth (bruxism)
  • Individuals with chronic tension headaches
  • Patients recovering from dental procedures
  • Anyone with unexplained jaw or facial pain

When Should You See a Doctor for TMJ Pain?

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience:

  • Severe pain that doesn't respond to over-the-counter medication
  • Difficulty opening or closing your mouth
  • Your jaw locks in an open or closed position
  • Severe swelling or fever
  • Pain that persists despite rest and home care

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TMJ massage painful?

How many sessions will I need?

Do you offer mobile TMJ massage in Seattle?

Can massage help with TMJ caused by stress?

Absolutely. Stress is a major contributor to TMJ. Our massage not only releases muscle tension but also calms the nervous system to reduce clenching.

When should I see a doctor for TMJ pain?

See a doctor if you experience severe pain, difficulty opening or closing your mouth, or if your jaw locks in an open or closed position. Seek immediate care if you have severe swelling or fever.

Can TMJ massage help with headaches?

Yes! TMJ-related tension headaches are common. Massage therapy can help release the tight jaw and neck muscles that often contribute to these headaches.
