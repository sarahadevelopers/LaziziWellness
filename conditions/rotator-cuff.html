What is the Rotator Cuff?

The rotator cuff is a group of four muscles and their tendons that surround the shoulder joint, keeping your upper arm bone securely in the shoulder socket. These muscles—supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor, and subscapularis—work together to provide stability and allow for a wide range of shoulder movements.

Common Rotator Cuff Injuries

  • Rotator Cuff Tendinitis: Inflammation of the tendons, often from overuse
  • Shoulder Impingement: Pinching of the tendons between bones
  • Rotator Cuff Tear: Partial or complete tearing of a tendon
  • Bursitis: Inflammation of the bursa sac in the shoulder
  • Frozen Shoulder: Adhesive capsulitis causing stiffness and pain

Common Symptoms

  • Shoulder pain: Especially when lifting the arm overhead
  • Weakness: Difficulty lifting or rotating the arm
  • Limited range of motion: Reduced ability to reach or stretch
  • Clicking or popping: Sounds when moving the shoulder
  • Night pain: Discomfort that disrupts sleep
  • Pain when lying on the affected side

When Should You See a Doctor for Shoulder Pain?

Seek immediate medical attention if you experience:

  • Sudden, severe shoulder pain
  • Inability to lift your arm
  • Visible deformity or swelling

  • Numbness or tingling in the arm or hand

Frequently Asked Questions

Can massage help a torn rotator cuff?

Massage can help manage pain and improve range of motion in cases of partial tears. For complete tears, it's best to work with your doctor and physical therapist. We can complement their treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

Many clients experience improvement after 3-4 sessions. A series of 6-8 sessions is often recommended for lasting results, depending on the severity of your condition.

Do you offer mobile rotator cuff massage in Seattle?

Yes! We bring the table to your home or office in Seattle, Bellevue, and surrounding King County areas. Perfect for those with limited mobility.

Is rotator cuff massage painful?

It can be slightly uncomfortable if the muscles are tight, but we always work within your comfort level. Most clients find it deeply relieving.

Can massage help with frozen shoulder?

Yes. Massage therapy can be very effective for frozen shoulder by releasing tight muscles and improving circulation. It's often combined with stretching exercises.

When should I see a doctor for shoulder pain?

See a doctor immediately if you experience sudden, severe pain, inability to lift your arm, visible deformity, or pain that persists despite rest and home care.
